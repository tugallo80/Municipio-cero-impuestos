> ÑANDÉ SCZ Digital is building a free education platform for public primary schools in Santa Cruz,
> Bolivia. Each child gets a tablet with their textbooks and a 3D learning world where school
> tasks become missions. A live, conversational AI tutor (two tutor personalities, "la profe" and
> "el profe", with a local Santa Cruz accent) guides students by voice with questions and stepwise
> hints, adapting to each child's level and pace. Teachers get a dashboard with each student's

> strict daily usage limits per student, and closed class-only worlds with no open chat.

> World Bank AI tutoring trial in Nigeria, 2025) shows AI tutoring can produce large gains. We
> want to bring that to Bolivian public schools.
